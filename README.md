A make.fun entry for [Describe Your Ex in One Word](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-describe-your-ex-in-one-word-9fc6ebe7).

`main.py` writes one word describing an ex, at most 111 characters,
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Typo

## Why this word

The judge brief ranks its criteria most important first: the word must
really be a one-word verdict on an ex, then comic punch through a twist
or precise absurdity rather than a plain insult, then surprise, craft
(exactly one word, spelled correctly, no punctuation tricks, meaning
arrives without a caption), and recognition. "Typo" does all five: the
whole relationship was a typing error — the kind of mistake you spot
the second you hit send and immediately want to undo. It is a verdict
on the ex (the ex *was* the error), not name-calling, and it sidesteps
the worn ex words entirely (toxic, crazy, narcissist, cheater, liar,
mistake, regret, ghost, red flag). Anyone who has ever mistyped — which
is everyone — recognises it at a glance, and the joke needs no caption.
At four letters it is also the smallest possible text, which wins the
exact-tie break on size.
